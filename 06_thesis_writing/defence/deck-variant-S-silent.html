02The gap: a production system that explains but cannot anticipate0:45–2:00 · A
  • Manifold AI ships a conversational assistant to Danish retailers and FMCG manufacturers

04The artefact: three layers, and one decision that carries SRQ23:15–4:45 · A → B
  • Forecasting substrate — five model families, one served per category, selected on cross-validated not test accuracy
  • Typed tool interface — JSON function calling, closed schema, two arguments
  • Bounded tool-using agent — orchestrates, validates, communicates; advises, never acts

The load-bearing decision: the language model never handles a feature vector. Feature construction stays server-side, versioned, identical on every call.

Figures: 06_architecture/figures/ch6_layered_architecture_v2.svg, then ch6_tool_interface_v1.svg.

Say

A "Three layers." → hand over → B "And the single decision that makes the forecast auditable rather than merely available: the model gets a forecast, not a feature vector. That is most of our SRQ2 contribution."

Handover — rehearse this

A finishes on the three layers; B opens on the server-side boundary. One sentence each, no pause, no "over to you".

Framing correction to own, not defend: a bounded tool-using agent, not a multi-agent system, in Sapkota et al.'s (2026) sense — fixed tool set, no delegation, no autonomous action. Ch9 §9.2.1 says so. If asked "what is agentic about it then?": tool-mediated reasoning under a schema, and the boundedness is what keeps it auditable and inside budget.
05SRQ1 in one slide — and the qualification we report rather than absorb4:45–6:00 · A
  • Both gradient boosters beat Ridge and ARIMA in all four categories
  • But a five-seed sweep changes which one is selected in every category — so they are statistically indistinguishable here
  • ⚠ Two of four categories are beaten outright by a parameter-free benchmark: seasonal naive on RTD (27.3 vs 30.1), Prophet on water (19.4)
  • Memory: fitting peaks at 34.5 MB against a 4096 MB ceiling — 0.84% of budget

Say

"Two things worth saying plainly. First, we do not name a winner between LightGBM and XGBoost, because the seed changes the answer — the difference between the families is smaller than the variation each produces on its own. Second, a forecast costing nothing to compute beats our substrate in two of four categories, and we report that rather than burying it."

Evidence

19_statistical_baselines.md, 20_seed_stability_p1.md, 15_substrate_resource_profile.md.

The memory answer, if challenged: the budget bound the selection space, not the runtime footprint. It excluded transformers and locally hosted LLMs before any was fitted. That the survivors sat far below the ceiling is a result, not a failed constraint. Concede openly.

Say

"A two-arm design would have confounded two different things: the value of giving an agent the firm's data at all, and the value of giving it a trained model rather than a sandbox. So every rung adds exactly one capability, and the B-to-C step is the thesis contribution."

[extra 30 s available here in this variant] "Three rungs are repeated inside the production platform. That matters more than it looks: the same intervention applied in two independently built agentic systems means a consistent effect cannot be an artefact of one evaluation harness. And the two settings fail differently on purpose — the general-purpose rungs are reproducible from our repository, an API key and the panel, while the production rungs are ecologically valid but cannot be re-executed by a reader. Neither property is sufficient alone, so the design is built so the two corroborate each other."

B → C: 51× cheaper, 21× faster, zero variance — and worse on the median.

Say

"Read the first increment first: giving the model the firm's own data moves median error from 502% to 2.9%. That is worth far more than anything above it on the ladder. Now the contribution step. Our artefact is worse on the median and better on the mean — the code-writing agent is better most of the time and occasionally much worse. What our model buys is 51 times the cost efficiency, 21 times the speed, and an identical answer every single time."

Do not let "the code arm won" stand bare

Always restate: at 39 months and 95 brands, a per-series ensemble beats a category-tuned booster on the median while costing 51× more, taking 21× longer, and producing no auditable tool call. The three advantages follow from one structural fact: a trained pipeline is work done once; an agent handed the same data repeats that work every session.

⚠ If they read the appendix: scenario D (code, production orchestrator) is the most accurate rung at 0.9% median — better than B. An orchestrator effect on the code path, not a point for our artefact. Say so.
08The negative result we chose to report10:00–11:15 · B
  • Reliability holds: every figure the agent reported matched the tool's figure, in every run
  • Traceability: implemented in full, evaluated in part — we say which
  • ⚠ Uncertainty is where we return a negative: the 90% interval spans 8 to 34× the quantity forecast
  • The confidence index built on it discriminates nothing — every forecast in every category tiers "Low"

Say

"Our interface delivers a truthful account of its own uncertainty rather than a useful one. The interval attains its coverage guarantee and is still too wide for a planner to act on for a single brand. Three alternative calibration schemes were implemented and measured; none improved coverage and width together in more than two of four categories. We report that as a finding rather than hiding it as an omission — and it is what makes the chapter's actual claim: what carries the reliability of a forecast is the measured track record travelling beside it, not the interval."

Why this slide earns its place in S

It is the most intellectually honest thing in the thesis and it is a designed negative result, not a defect. It also pre-empts the obvious attack on SRQ2 by making it first.

If asked why the index shipped at all: published experiment runs are scored against it, and it cannot be repaired by re-weighting because both terms are functions of a quantile fixed within a category. An unexpected defence — in the combined scenarios both agents departed from the model's forecast and cited its confidence tier and interval width as the reason. A constant "low" was, on that brand, the honest signal, and it changed behaviour.

Say

"Design science says an instantiation becomes a contribution only when the knowledge it embodies is stated separately from it. Each of these is at the level of a problem class, not our artefact, and each is falsifiable against the evidence column — we assert them because something in our evaluation would have come out differently had they not held."

Expect the methodology question here

"You completed one design cycle, so these are derived, not validated." Ch10 §10.4 concedes exactly that. The answer: one cycle is what a thesis-scale DSR study delivers; the principles are stated so they can be falsified, and the evidence column is what makes each one testable rather than asserted.
10Closing: the result that most deserves to survive12:45–14:30 · B
  • The extension is feasible inside a small provider's budget — 231 MB end to end
  • The constraint binds at design time, not run time
  • A language model with data and a sandbox is a stronger forecaster than the literature grants it
  • So a dedicated predictive layer must be justified on reproducibility, cost and auditability — not on error alone

A forecast becomes usable not when it carries an interval, but when it carries evidence of how far its model has been right before.

Say

"The result we did not set out to find is the one we would keep. We set out to show a dedicated forecasting layer was needed, and what we can actually defend is narrower and more useful: it earns its place on properties the error metric does not see."

[extra 30 s available here in this variant] "It is worth being precise about what that means for a practitioner, because it is the part we would want to survive the thesis. If you need the same question answered the same way every time, cheaply enough to ask on every query, and auditable afterwards, the dedicated model is the configuration to deploy. If you want a one-off analysis and a human will read the working, the code-writing configuration gives a better typical answer and an occasional very poor one — which is acceptable under review and not acceptable unattended. That is a deployment rule a two-arm comparison could not have produced."
