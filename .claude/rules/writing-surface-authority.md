# Writing Surface Authority

Thesis prose has exactly **one** authoritative home. Every other surface is
derived, and derived surfaces are never edited by hand.

## Quick Reference

| Surface | Role | Editable? |
|---------|------|-----------|
| OneDrive `.docx` (shared with Enrico) | **Authoritative prose** | Yes — this is where prose is written |
| `05_thesis_writing/docx-exported-snapshots/` | Read-only mirror; the diffable record | **Never** — regenerated by `thesis_snapshot.py` |
| `05_thesis_writing/sections-drafts/*.md` | Planning: bullets, structure, status, provenance | Yes — but **no prose** |
| `05_thesis_writing/sections-final/` | **Gone** (archived 2026-09-01) | — |

## Why prose lives in exactly one place

Until 2026-09-01 the prose existed twice: in the OneDrive `.docx` and, at 76–97 %
similarity, in `sections-drafts/*.md`. They agreed only because nobody had edited
either since the export.

That agreement was luck, and it was about to expire. Review comments anchor to
`.docx` text. Acting on one means editing the `.docx`; the `.md` then silently
falls behind, and nothing in the repo records which of the two is current. The
first substantive edit would have produced two divergent copies with no authority
rule to resolve them.

A second copy is also not a backup. A copy that is only *sometimes* current is
worse than none, because it cannot be trusted without a diff — and the snapshot
already provides a greppable, diffable copy that **cannot** drift, because it is
generated rather than maintained.

## What `sections-drafts/` is for

Not a prose mirror — a planning surface. These files carry things the `.docx` has
never held and should not hold:

- status headers (`Status: SKELETON`, `REVISED 2026-08-23`)
- provenance and verification notes (e.g. Ch6's withdrawn ≤15 % accuracy target,
  the P0041 citation-verification state, the stale `fig4_ram_budget` flag)
- open questions and TODOs
- bullet skeletons awaiting approval for prose

This is why the strip **kept** headings, blockquotes, lists and tables while
dropping paragraphs. Ch6 retained 3,308 of 6,155 words on exactly this basis;
Ch1, which was pure prose, fell to 498.

## How to apply

| Situation | Do |
|-----------|-----|
| Writing or revising prose | Edit the OneDrive `.docx` |
| Acting on a review comment | Edit the `.docx`, then re-run the snapshot |
| Planning a section, tracking status | Edit `sections-drafts/*.md` — bullets only |
| Checking drift | Diff `docx-exported-snapshots/` between dates |
| Tempted to paste prose into a draft | **Don't.** That recreates the problem this rule removed |

Bullets-first still applies: a new section starts as bullets in
`sections-drafts/`, and becomes prose in the `.docx` only after human approval.
