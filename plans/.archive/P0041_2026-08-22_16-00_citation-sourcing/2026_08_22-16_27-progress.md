### The distinction that matters

The citation register asks *"does a source exist for this claim?"*. Chapter 2 raises
the opposite question: **"the source exists and is cited correctly — but does our own
evidence now contradict what we said it implies?"**

Four such disconnections found. None is a citation error; all are unfulfilled
"therefore" statements:

| ID | Ch2 says | Reality |
|----|----------|---------|
| D1 | M4 + Ahrens support combining models | **No ensemble was built** — single model served |
| D2 | Ceran et al.: 15% MAPE is the acceptable benchmark | Met on 2 of 4 categories **on WMAPE**; RTD is ~2x over. **Which metric Ceran uses is unverified** |
| D3 | Klee & Xia: stability is a production criterion; SRQ1 evaluates it | **Stability was never measured** — no CV-across-runs column exists |
| D4 | Ma et al.: no single model dominates | **Corroborated, and more strongly than claimed** |

**D3 is the most serious**: a promised SRQ1 deliverable that does not exist. Cheap to
fix (refit with N seeds, report coefficient of variation) and would settle the F51
seed-stability question at the same time.

**D2 has a single question that decides it**: does Ceran et al. (2024) mean plain MAPE,
median MAPE, or WMAPE? On WMAPE the thesis meets the target on two categories; on
median MAPE it meets it on none. **First NotebookLM task.**

### Four further validation areas identified

1. **Preprint concentration** — 15 of 39 sources. Which arguments load-bear on
   non-peer-reviewed work? Have any been published since (free quality upgrade)?
2. **The "closest paper" claim** — Bürger & Pauli (2024, EAAI) is the single most
   load-bearing citation; the entire novelty argument rests on it. Verify it says what
   is attributed, **and that nothing closer has appeared since.**
3. **The five gaps G1-G5 are assertions of absence** — the hardest claims to support,
   defeated by one counterexample. **G2 and G3 are the exposed ones.** NotebookLM
   should be instructed to actively try to REFUTE them.
4. **Conformal prediction is absent from Ch2 entirely** — yet every served forecast
   carries a split-conformal interval. This is a *literature* gap, not just a citation
   gap: a section is needed, not a reference.
