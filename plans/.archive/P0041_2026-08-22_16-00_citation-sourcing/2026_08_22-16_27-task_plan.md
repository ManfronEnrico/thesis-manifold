# P0041 — Citation sourcing register

## Purpose

Every "best practice" or methodological decision in the thesis needs a real,
verifiable academic source. This plan is the **register of which claims have a
prospective source and which do not**, maintained continuously alongside the
modelling work rather than reconstructed at writing time.

**Intended use:** the unsourced and unverified claims are exported to NotebookLM,
which is well suited to finding real papers and validating whether a suggested
source actually contains the recommendation attributed to it.

## The problem this exists to prevent

During the 2026-08-22 session an assistant asserted that *"convention for
hyperparameter search is 50-200 trials"*. **There is no such source.** It was
practitioner folklore stated with unwarranted confidence. Had it reached the thesis
with a fabricated citation attached, a grader checking the reference would have found
it did not support the claim.

That failure mode -- a plausible-sounding claim acquiring a plausible-sounding
citation -- is the specific risk this register manages. **The default assumption for
any entry here is UNVERIFIED until someone has opened the actual source.**

## Status taxonomy

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `VERIFIED` | Source opened, claim confirmed to appear there, page/section noted |
| `PROSPECTIVE` | A specific source is suggested but nobody has checked it |
| `UNSOURCED` | No source identified; needs research |
| `UNSOURCEABLE` | Investigated and no academic source exists; must be argued empirically or dropped |

**Nothing is `VERIFIED` on an assistant's say-so.** An LLM can name a real work and
describe its contents plausibly while being wrong about whether a specific claim
appears in it. Verification means a human opened the source.

## Working rule for all future sessions

**When any session makes a methodological claim that would need a citation, add it
to `findings.md` here in the same session.** Do not defer -- the claim will be
forgotten and will surface in the prose without provenance.

When an assistant proposes a source, it is recorded as `PROSPECTIVE`, never
`VERIFIED`, regardless of how confident the proposal sounded.
