## Zero-Sales Characterisation across 101 brands

- Brands classified by the position of zero-sales months in their series: leading zeros preceding market entry, trailing zeros following exit, intermittent zeros interspersed through an active period, or none.
- The distinction is substantive. Leading and trailing zeros denote periods in which the brand was not on the market, whereas intermittent zeros are realised demand of zero and are part of the process being modelled; discarding the latter would bias the target distribution upward.
- Intermittent demand also constrains the choice of accuracy metric, since percentage-error measures are undefined when the actual value is zero (Hyndman and Koehler, 2006).
