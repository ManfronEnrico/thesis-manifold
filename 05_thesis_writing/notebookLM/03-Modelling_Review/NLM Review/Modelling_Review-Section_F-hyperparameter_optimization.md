### 2. Detailed Methodological Analysis

#### A. Mathematical Formulation of the Tree-Structured Parzen Estimator (TPE)
Unlike standard Sequential Model-Based Optimization (SMBO) which directly models the response surface $p(yert x)$ (typically via a Gaussian Process), the TPE algorithm models the input space density conditioned on the objective performance, $p(xert y)$, and the prior performance $p(y)$. 

The configuration space $\mathcal{X}$ is structured as a generative process. The TPE models $p(xert y)$ by splitting the observed history $\mathcal{H}$ of trials into two distinct non-parametric densities based on a quantile threshold $y^*$:

$$p(xert y) = egin{cases} l(x) & 	ext{if } y < y^* \ g(x) & 	ext{if } y \geq y^* \end{cases}$$

Here, $l(x)$ represents the Parzen window density of configurations that achieved an objective score below $y^*$ (recalling that we are minimizing a loss), and $g(x)$ is the density formed by all other configurations. The threshold $y^*$ is chosen as a specific quantile $\gamma$ of the observed $y$ values, such that:

$$p(y < y^*) = \gamma$$

To maximize the Expected Improvement (EI) over the current best score $y^*$, the acquisition function is formulated as:

$$EI_{y^*}(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{y^*} (y^* - y) p(yert x) dy = rac{\gamma y^* l(x) - l(x) \int_{-\infty}^{y^*} p(y) dy}{\gamma l(x) + (1-\gamma)g(x)}$$

$$EI_{y^*}(x) \propto \left(\gamma + rac{g(x)}{l(x)}(1-\gamma)ight)^{-1}$$

To maximize this expression, the algorithm draws candidates from $l(x)$ and evaluates them under the ratio $g(x) / l(x)$, returning the candidate $x^*$ that minimizes this ratio (i.e., has high probability under the "good" density $l(x)$ and low probability under the "poor" density $g(x)$).

### 3. Critical Sourcing Recommendations for the Thesis

1.  **Refute the folklore of fixed trial counts:**
    The thesis draft must delete any claims stating that "50-200 trials represent an established standard in the HPO literature." Instead, frame the trial budget as an empirical, data-driven choice. Plot the validation metric across iterations and show where it plateaus, demonstrating to examiners that you monitored convergence objectively.
2.  **Separate Software Implementations from Math Foundations:**
    *   Do **not** cite Akiba et al. (2019) for the mathematical definition or derivation of TPE. Use Akiba et al. (2019) to cite the *use of the Optuna framework* and its "define-by-run" API design.
    *   Cite Bergstra et al. (2011) to justify the *mathematical formulation* of TPE.
3.  **Acknowledge GP and TPE limitations:**
    Sequential optimization algorithms assume the response surface exhibits learnable structure. As noted by Bergstra et al. (2011), SMBO can perform worse than random search if the surrogate model $p(yert x)$ (or $p(xert y)$) is misspecified or if it extracts structure that traps the search in a local optimum. This limitation must be explicitly discussed in the HPO section of the thesis.
